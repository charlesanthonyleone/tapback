FAQ

Good questions.

Everything people ask about Pocket Nag. Still stuck? Visit support.

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. It hears about purchases through your bank's own transaction alerts, so you never share a bank login or connect an account.

How does it work on Android vs iPhone?

On Android it reads your bank's purchase notifications. On iPhone, Apple Pay taps trigger it instantly, and other purchases come in through your bank's email alerts.

What does it actually read?

Only purchase alerts: merchant, amount and time. It doesn't read your messages, and your data is never sold. There are no ads.

Is this a bank?

No. Pocket Nag doesn't hold money, move money or issue cards. It works alongside the bank you already have.

Can I make it stop talking?

Yes. Choose “Off” for silent tracking, set quiet hours, or switch to text-only notifications.

Does it work with Siri or other assistants?

That's the plan. The voice can come from your phone's built-in speech or an assistant you already use.

When is the Glass Card coming?

It's an early concept. Join the waitlist to hear first.

Is my demo data saved anywhere?

The website demo keeps everything in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server. Use “Reset week” on the demo to clear it.

Which banks does it work with?

Any bank that sends purchase notifications or email alerts should work, since Pocket Nag reads the alert rather than connecting to the bank.

Can I try it today?

Yes. Try the website demo, or follow the setup guide to run the prototype on your own phone.
