Troubleshooting

The demo doesn't speak

Check that your volume is up and your phone isn't on silent. Some browsers need you to tap once before they allow sound. If your browser has no speech voices, the demo still shows the text.

My iPhone Shortcut doesn't run on Apple Pay

Open Shortcuts → Automation, check the Wallet automation is set to “Run Immediately”, and that your card is selected. See the iPhone section of the setup guide.

Android doesn't react to purchases

Make sure the automation app has notification access, and that your bank app's notifications are turned on.

It's too loud / too often

Choose the “Off” personality for silent tracking, or set quiet hours.
