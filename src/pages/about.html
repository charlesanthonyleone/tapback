About

A loving voice for every wallet.

Tapback exists to make money awareness feel less like homework and more like a friend who keeps you honest.

Founder

Roseann Mary Geiger

Roseann is a mom, and the founder of Tapback.

She noticed something most of us know: the money that disappears isn't usually the big purchases. It's the small, easy ones that nobody mentions. Charts and spreadsheets tell you about it later, after it's already gone.

So she asked a different question: what if your wallet just said something, right there, the moment you spent? Not cruelly. The way a mom would. Warm, funny, and honest enough to make you think twice.

— Roseann Mary Geiger, Founder

Mission

Make every purchase a moment of awareness.

One short, funny line at the moment you spend does what a monthly chart can't: it helps you notice.

Vision

A wallet that cares about you.

From the app on your phone today to the Glass Card tomorrow, Tapback aims to put a kind, honest voice right where money moves.
