Pricing FAQ

Money questions (ironic, we know).

Is the free plan really free forever?

Yes. The free plan includes every personality, the full line library, the visit counter and the widget. There's no trial timer.

What does Pro cost?

The planned price is $2.99 a month. Pricing may change before launch; anyone on the waitlist will hear first.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, any time. You'd simply go back to the free plan.

Does the Glass Card cost extra?

The Glass Card is a concept that's still being designed, so it doesn't have a price yet. Reserving a spot is free and commits you to nothing.

Do you make money from my data?

No. There are no ads and your data is never sold. Pro subscriptions are how Tapback plans to pay its bills.
