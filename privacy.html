Privacy policy

Plain-English draft of how Pocket Nag intends to handle data.

DRAFT TEMPLATE, pending legal review. This is not a final legal document and does not name a legal entity. It must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before Pocket Nag launches.

Last updated: draft, not in effect.

1. What this covers

This draft describes the Pocket Nag website, the website demo and the Pocket Nag app prototype ("Pocket Nag", "we").

2. What the website collects

The website has no accounts and no analytics. The waitlist form and the demo store data only in your own browser's local storage. That data is not transmitted to us. You can clear it by clearing your browser's site data.

3. What the app is designed to read

The app is designed to read only purchase alerts: merchant name, amount and time. It is not designed to read personal messages, and it never asks for bank login details.

4. How data would be used

Purchase details are used to choose and speak a line, keep a weekly visit count, and show your weekly total. Pro features that use AI would process purchase details to write a line.

5. What we will never do

  • Sell your data.
  • Show ads.
  • Ask for your bank password.

6. Your choices

You can switch the voice off, set quiet hours, and delete your data at any time.
