The App

A voice for every purchase.

Pocket Nag listens for the purchase alerts your bank already sends, then says one short line about what you just bought. It counts your visits too, so the fourth McDonald's of the week sounds very different from the first.

Try the live demoSetup guide (PDF)

How it works

Three steps. No bank login, ever.

  1. 1

    You tap or pay

    Pay the way you always do. You don't need a new card or a new bank account.

  2. 2

    Your bank's alert tells it

    Your bank already sends a transaction alert for each purchase, and that's how it hears about it. You never connect your bank or share a bank login.

  3. 3

    It nags (nicely)

    It says a short line about the purchase, in the personality you picked.

🤖 Android

It reads the purchase notifications your bank app already shows you.

🍎 iPhone

Apple Pay taps trigger it instantly. Other purchases come in through your bank's email alerts.

It escalates

The more you go back, the louder it gets.

  1. 1st visit“A McDonald's run? Bold. Respect.”
  2. 2nd visitA raised eyebrow.
  3. 3rd visit“THIRD time at McDonald's this week?! Are you collecting the toys?”
  4. 4th visit+“DAMN, FOR THE 4TH TIME THIS WEEK?! EAT SOMETHING HEALTHY FOR ONCE!”

Counts reset every week. Lines come from a library of 160 written for 8 spending categories.

Personalities

Pick who's talking.

Five moods. Switch any time. Click one to hear it in the demo.

Set it up today

Working prototype on your own phone.

🍎 iPhone

  1. Build one Shortcut that picks a line and speaks it.
  2. Add a Wallet automation so every Apple Pay tap runs it.
  3. Optional: let bank email alerts trigger it for other purchases.

About 20 minutes with the guide.

🤖 Android

  1. Install an automation app (MacroDroid).
  2. Import the Pocket Nag script.
  3. Point it at your bank app's notifications.

About 15 minutes with the guide.
