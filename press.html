Boilerplate

Pocket Nag is a voice-first money awareness app that comments on every purchase, the moment it happens, in a personality the user chooses: encouraging, sassy, strict, grandma or silent. It hears about purchases through the transaction alerts people already get from their bank, so it needs no bank login. Pocket Nag is free, with an optional Pro plan. The company is also designing the Glass Card, a concept clear-glass card that holds all of a user's cards and features One-Tap Unlock. Pocket Nag was founded by Roseann Mary Geiger.
