# Voice clips
The MP3 files in this folder were generated offline with **Kokoro-82M v1.0** (hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, ONNX export onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX), which is licensed under **Apache-2.0**, including its voicepacks. Voices used: af_heart (Gentle Coach), af_bella (Sassy Friend), bm_george (Strict Accountant) and bf_emma (Grandma).
Regenerate them with `tools/render_voices.mjs`. The in-browser engine uses kokoro-js (Apache-2.0).
