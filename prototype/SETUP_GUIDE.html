👛 Pocket Nag

Your wallet finally has something to say.

When you pay with your phone, it says something funny out loud, like "Back at Starbucks? Third time this week!". It's all in good fun, and the jokes are never mean.

Setup takes about 5 minutes. Find your phone type below and follow the numbered steps. If something doesn't match exactly, don't worry: Charles can help over the phone.

Which phone do I have? If there's an apple logo on the back, it's an iPhone → Part 1. Otherwise (Samsung, Google Pixel, Motorola…) it's Android → Part 2.

Part 1 · iPhone

You need: an iPhone with your card in Apple Wallet (the one you use to pay by tapping), and the link Charles texted you.

Step A: Add the Pocket Nag shortcut

  1. Open the link Charles sent (it starts with icloud.com/shortcuts).
  2. The Shortcuts app opens. Tap Add Shortcut.
  3. To try it, open Shortcuts (white and pink-blue icon), find Pocket Nag and tap it once. Your phone should say a joke about McDonald's. 🎉

Step B: Make it run when you pay

  1. In Shortcuts, tap Automation at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Tap + (top right) or New Automation.
  3. Scroll down and tap Transaction. On newer phones it's called Wallet.
  4. Make sure your card is checked. Leave everything else as it is.
  5. Tap Run Immediately. Then turn the Notify When Run switch off (it turns grey).
  6. Tap Next, then New Blank Automation (or Create New Shortcut).
  7. Tap Add Action, type Dictionary in the search box and tap it.
  8. Tap Add new item → Text. In the left box type merchant. Tap the right box, then tap Shortcut Input in the bar above the keyboard. Tap the blue Shortcut Input word and choose Merchant.
  9. Do the same again: Add new item → Text, type amount on the left, choose Shortcut Input → Amount on the right.
  10. Tap the search box at the bottom, type Run Shortcut and tap it. Tap the blue word Shortcut and choose Pocket Nag.
  11. Tap Done. That's it! Next time you tap to pay, listen. 🔊

Part 2 · Android

You need: Google Wallet set up for tap-to-pay, and the free app MacroDroid.

Good to know: Android phones can't hear the tap itself. Pocket Nag listens for Google Wallet's "you just paid" message instead, so the joke may come a little after you pay, not at the exact moment.
  1. Open the Play Store, search for MacroDroid and tap Install.
  2. Open MacroDroid. When it asks for Notification access, tap Allow, find MacroDroid in the list and switch it on.
  3. Open Google Wallet → tap your picture (top right) → Notifications → make sure Transactions is on.
  4. Charles will send you the Pocket Nag macro, or set it up with you. It takes him about 3 minutes.
  5. In MacroDroid, make sure the Pocket Nag switch is on (green).
  6. Pay for something small with your phone and listen. 🔊

If it doesn't talk: troubleshooting

ProblemTry this
iPhone: a box pops up asking "Run?"Shortcuts → Automation → tap the automation → choose Run Immediately.
iPhone: you see a banner but hear nothingTurn Notify When Run off (same screen as above). Then check the volume below.
No sound at allPress the volume-up button on the side while nothing is playing. The shortcut also turns the volume up itself.
iPhone silent switch / Silent modeIf the little switch on the side shows orange, or Silent mode is on (iPhone 15 and later: Action button), spoken words may be muted. Flip it back to ring.
Android: silent or Do Not DisturbSwipe down from the top of the screen and turn Do Not Disturb off. Turn the media volume up.
Android: nothing happensSettings → Notifications → Notification access → MacroDroid on. In MacroDroid, check that the macro is on. Some phones need Battery → Unrestricted for MacroDroid.
It says the wrong store nameThat's normal sometimes. The store's official name can look strange (like "SQ *JOE'S CAFE").
Jokes about paying with a plastic card?It only works when you pay with your phone, not a physical card.
Want it to stopiPhone: Shortcuts → Automation → swipe left on it → Delete. Android: switch the macro off in MacroDroid.

Pocket Nag never sees your bank login. Everything stays on your phone. 💜
